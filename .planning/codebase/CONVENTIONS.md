# Architectural & Design Conventions

## Zero-AI-Vibe Manifesto
- **No Emojis:** Strictly prohibited in UI, table cells, buttons, badges, and alerts.
- **Iconography:** Precision monochrome vector icons from `lucide-react` with 1.25px stroke.
- **Rhythm:** Strict 8px/4px spacing scale (`p-3`, `p-4`, `p-6`, `p-8`, `gap-4`, `gap-6`).
- **Typography Scale:** Strictly constrained to 4 distinct operational tiers:
  - `caption`: 12px / 18px line-height
  - `body`: 14px / 24px line-height
  - `subhead`: 18px / 26px line-height
  - `title`: 28px / 34px line-height
- **Color Palette:**
  - Canvas: `#FAF9F6`
  - Ink: `#18181B`
  - Rules: `#E4E4E7`
  - Accent: `#B58544` (Antique Brass)
